# THE ORIGINAL — From the Cashew Capital, Palasa

> **"Quality on our side, trust from yours."**

**The Original** is an authentic, luxury cashew brand founded by **CH Dhana Vardhan**, native to Palasa, Andhra Pradesh. Built with the conviction to share genuine Palasa-grown cashews across India with world-class packaging, elevated presentation, and uncompromised origin freshness.

---

## 🌾 The Palasa Provenance

Nestled where the Eastern Ghats meet the Bay of Bengal, Palasa’s mineral-dense red laterite soil, tropical humidity, and coastal sea breezes form an ancient microclimate celebrated as **India’s Cashew Capital**. 

Unlike mass commercial brands that sell 6-to-12-month-old warehouse cold storage stock coated with artificial stabilizers, **The Original** operates strictly on **factory-fresh, unaged stock**: freshly roasted, rigorously sorted, and sealed immediately at origin to preserve natural sweet oils and satisfying crunch.

---

## 🏆 The Master Curator / Relabeller Model

We are proud **selective relabellers and curators** rather than a single-factory processor:
1. **Multi-Factory Inspection**: We audit and evaluate lots across multiple processing units in the Palasa belt.
2. **Cherry-Picked Quality**: We select only the finest batches meeting strict caliber, moisture, and sweetness criteria.
3. **Master Hand-Sorting**: Every kernel is triple-sorted to guarantee uniform shape and zero scorched or broken pieces.
4. **Heritage Luxury Packaging**: Nitrogen-sealed, multi-layer aroma-barrier pouches designed to bring pride to Palasa on any festive or dining table.

## 🥜 Harvest Catalog & Signatures

### Whole & Split Calibers
- **Grade W180 (Jumbo King)**: The rarest and largest king cashew of Palasa. Rich ivory density and creamy mouthfeel. *(250g, 500g)*
- **Grade W220 (Selected Jumbo)**: Generously sized whole cashews with classic snap and coastal sweetness. *(250g, 500g)*
- **Grade W320 (Classic Benchmark)**: The timeless standard of Palasa harvests for everyday nourishment. *(250g, 500g)*
- **Palasa Raw Cashew Nuts / Skin Cashews (Pottu Pappu)**: Authentic unpeeled roasted cashews with protective skins intact. Nutty depth, extra crunch, and high natural fiber. *(250g, 500g)*
- **Grade JH & SJH (Clean Splits)**: Pristine split halves and small kernels for traditional sweets, curries, and culinary garnishing. *(250g, 500g)*

### Confectionery & Dry Fruits

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Framework**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, Turbopack)
- **Styling**: Pure Vanilla CSS (`app/globals.css`) with curated design tokens (`--navy-deep`, `--gold-soft`, `--paper`, `--cream`)
- **Typography**: Single clean typeface family [Outfit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit) via Google Fonts
- **Features**:
  - **Full-Screen Hero Video** with layered typography and direct actions.
  - **About Us Route (`/about`)** with Palasa drone footage, founder spotlight, and transparency manifesto.
  - **Products Catalog (`/products`)** with visual category tiles, sidebar filtering (search, categories, sizes, prices, sorting), dynamic size toggles, Add to Cart, and Buy Now.
  - **Slide-Over Cart Drawer** with item steppers and one-click WhatsApp order formatting.
  - **Contact Us Route (`/contact`)** with interactive inquiry form, direct WhatsApp integration, and embedded Google Maps iframe.

## 🚀 Getting Started Locally

npm run dev
