The soil of Palasa

An ancient coastal microclimate where India's cashew story began.

In the northeastern pocket of Andhra Pradesh, where the Eastern Ghats roll into the Bay of Bengal, lies Palasa. Red laterite soil, tropical ocean humidity, and gentle sea breezes create an ecosystem unlike any other cashew growing region in the world.

Most commercial cashews travel through multiple middlemen, warehouse floors, and high-heat ovens that sap the kernel of its delicate sweet oils. By the time they reach grocery shelves, they need added vegetable oils and artificial stabilizers to mimic freshness.

The Original was founded on a simple conviction: harvest strictly from family orchards in Palasa, roast with gentle indirect heat, and vacuum-seal directly in small batches at origin. The result is pure, buttery crunch as nature intended.
